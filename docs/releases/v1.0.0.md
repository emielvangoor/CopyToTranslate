**Copy Spanish. Read English. Stay in your flow.**

CopyToTranslate 1.0 brings automatic Spanish-to-English translation to your Mac's clipboard. Copy text in any app and a small English translation appears in the top-right corner, without switching apps or taking keyboard focus.

## What's included

### Translate entirely on your Mac

- Spanish detection and English translation use Apple's on-device frameworks. There are no accounts, API keys, subscriptions, or cloud translation requests.
- Emoji and symbols are excluded from language detection to help recognize informal Spanish messages. The original passage, including its emoji, is used for translation.
- A manual **Translate Clipboard as Spanish** command handles short or ambiguous phrases that automatic detection may skip.

### Read and copy without interrupting your work

- One compact, scrollable translation card appears at the top right of the display containing your pointer.
- **Copy English** copies the complete translation. Your original clipboard remains unchanged until you choose that action.
- A visible **five-second countdown** shows when the card will close. Hover to pause it; move away to resume the remaining time. Reduce Motion uses a static timer icon.
- New clipboard content dismisses the previous result, so an older translation does not remain on screen.

### A quiet menu bar companion

- Pause or resume translation, try the built-in example, and open Setup from the menu bar.
- Enable **Launch at login** to start quietly whenever you sign in. This uses macOS's native Login Items setting and can be switched off again in Setup.
- Monitoring pauses during sleep, screen lock, and inactive sessions. Startup and resume ignore clipboard content that was already there.
- Includes a custom macOS app icon and an MIT license.

## Download and first-time setup

**Requirements:** Apple silicon Mac (M-series), macOS 15 Sequoia or later.

1. Download **CopyToTranslate-v1.0.0-macos-arm64.zip** below and unzip it.
2. Move **CopyToTranslate.app** to **Applications**, then open it.
3. Click **Enable Translation** and approve Apple's Spanish and English language downloads if prompted. The initial downloads need an internet connection; subsequent translations run locally.
4. Allow clipboard access if macOS asks. Copy a Spanish sentence or click **Try Example**.
5. Optionally turn on **Launch at login** in Setup.

The app has no Dock icon. Closing Setup leaves it running in the menu bar.

## macOS first-launch prompt

This release is **ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple**. macOS may block the first launch. If you trust the download, try opening the app once, then use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. [Apple explains this process here](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).

The attached `.sha256` file lets you verify the downloaded ZIP. Source archives are also available below.

## Current limits

- Spanish → English is the only supported language pair, and downloadable builds target Apple silicon.
- Very short or uncertain text may not trigger automatically. Empty/non-text content, standalone URLs and email addresses, and selections over 10,000 characters are skipped.
- Known confidential clipboard markers are respected, but not all apps mark sensitive text. There is no clipboard history, analytics, or saved translation log.
- Translation quality depends on Apple's models. There is no online fallback or built-in app updater.

## Validation

The release workflow runs 15 automated tests, builds the app, checks its signature, and verifies the extracted ZIP and checksum before publishing. Clipboard detection, actual translation, Copy English, the countdown, the icon, and login-item registration were also checked locally on an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 15.6.

To update from an earlier build, quit CopyToTranslate, replace the app at the same location, reopen it, and check **Setup → Launch at login**.
